Our Mission

Every child in Nigeria deserves access to a great teacher. 2tors.ng exists to make that possible — connecting families with verified, qualified private tutors for home visits and online sessions, and giving parents the visibility they need to track their children's progress.

We built 2tors.ng because we know how difficult it is to find a reliable tutor in Nigeria. Too many parents rely on word-of-mouth, without any way to verify qualifications, track sessions or see progress. We're changing that.

Our Values

What drives every decision we make at 2tors.ng

Trust & Safety

Every tutor is manually verified. Children's data is handled with the highest standards of privacy and safeguarding. Parents control every permission — always.

Quality Teaching

We hold tutors to a high standard — not just on paper, but in practice. Progress reports, attendance records and parent feedback keep quality measurable and visible.

Impact at Scale

We are Nigeria-first, but we are building a platform that can serve every African family who deserves access to a qualified educator. Every child counts.
