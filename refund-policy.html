Refund Policy Our commitment to your satisfaction

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

At 2tors.ng, we stand by the quality of our tutoring services. If you are not completely satisfied with your tutoring experience, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your first session. This applies to all subscription plans.

Refund Terms

Refunds are processed under the following conditions: (1) Full refund for unused sessions within 30 days of purchase; (2) Pro-rated refund for partially used subscriptions minus the cost of completed sessions; (3) No refunds requested after 30 days of purchase, but unused session credits may be applied to future subscriptions.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team at support@2tors.ng.com with your account email and reason for the refund request. Alternatively, submit a support ticket through your account dashboard. We process all refund requests within 5-7 business days.

Single Session Refunds

If you are dissatisfied with a single tutoring session, you may request a refund or a free replacement session. This policy is valid for 7 days after the session date. Refunded session amounts depend on your subscription plan rate.

Tutor Cancellations

If your tutor cancels a session with less than 24 hours notice, you will receive a full credit for that session. If a tutor repeatedly cancels, you will be matched with a different tutor at no additional cost.

Exceptions

Scholarship-funded accounts are subject to separate refund terms as outlined in the scholarship agreement. Refund terms may vary for enterprise and school district accounts.
