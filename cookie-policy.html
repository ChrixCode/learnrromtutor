Cookie Policy How we use cookies

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, enhance your browsing experience, and provide analytical data to the website operators.

How We Use Cookies

2tors.ng uses cookies for the following purposes: (1) Essential cookies that are necessary for the platform to function, including authentication and security; (2) Preference cookies that remember your settings and language choices; (3) Analytics cookies that help us understand how you use our platform so we can improve it; (4) Marketing cookies that help us deliver relevant content and advertisements.

Types of Cookies We Use

Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. These are essential for the login system and platform functionality. Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until you manually delete them. They remember your preferences for future visits.

Third-Party Cookies

We use trusted third-party services including Google Analytics, Stripe for payments, and Intercom for customer support. These services may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third parties.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification before a cookie is set. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect platform functionality and prevent you from using certain features.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our cookie policy, please contact us at privacy@2tors.ng.com.
