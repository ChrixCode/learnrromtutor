About Dr. Okafor

I am a PhD-qualified Mathematics tutor with 6 years of experience teaching JSS, SSS and JAMB students in Lagos. I specialise in making abstract concepts tangible through real-world examples and structured problem-solving techniques.

Having taught over 200 students, I pride myself on identifying each learner's specific gaps and tailoring every session to address those directly. My students have consistently improved their grades by at least one full grade level within two months.

I am patient, punctual, well-prepared for every session and committed to helping every child reach their full potential.
