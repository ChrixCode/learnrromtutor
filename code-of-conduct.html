Code of Conduct Creating a respectful learning community

At 2tors.ng, we are committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for all users. This Code of Conduct applies to all students, tutors, and staff interacting on our platform. Violations may result in warning, suspension, or permanent removal from the platform.

1. Respect and Inclusivity

Treat everyone with dignity and respect. Discrimination, harassment, hate speech, or bullying of any kind based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, or any other characteristic is strictly prohibited.

2. Academic Integrity

Tutors provide guidance, explanations, and support to help students learn. Tutors must not complete assignments or exams on behalf of students. Students must submit their own work and use tutoring as a learning aid, not a shortcut.

3. Professional Communication

Maintain professional and courteous communication in all interactions. Use appropriate language, be punctual for sessions, and provide constructive feedback. Personal contact information should not be shared outside the platform.

4. Privacy and Confidentiality

Session recordings, materials, and personal information shared on the platform are confidential. Do not share, record, or distribute session content without explicit consent from all participants.

5. Platform Integrity

Do not attempt to manipulate ratings, reviews, or the tutor matching system. Creating multiple accounts, falsifying credentials, or engaging in fraudulent activity will result in immediate account termination.

6. Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct, report it immediately via the support system or by emailing conduct@2tors.ng.com. All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially.

By using 2tors.ng, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct. Together, we create a positive and productive learning environment for everyone.
