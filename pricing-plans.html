How the 10% Commission Works

When a tutor sets their rate at ₦5,000 per session, the parent pays ₦5,000. 2tors.ng retains ₦500 (10%) as a platform fee. The tutor receives ₦4,500 in their monthly payout.

There are no signup fees, no monthly subscriptions and no hidden charges. Tutors are only charged when they successfully complete a session. Minimum payout threshold: ₦10,000.

Pricing Questions

When does the tutor pay the commission?
The 10% platform commission is deducted automatically from each completed session before payout. Tutors do not pay upfront — the commission is only applied when a session is marked complete and payment is confirmed.
Is there any signup fee for tutors?
No. Registration is completely free. The only cost to tutors is the 10% commission on completed sessions. There are no monthly fees, no listing fees and no charge for the verification process.
Do parents pay anything to use the platform?
No. Creating an account, adding children, browsing tutors, sending booking requests and viewing progress reports are all free for parents. Parents only pay the tutor's session fee — set by the tutor — when a booking is confirmed and a session takes place.
How and when do tutors receive payouts?
Tutors can request a payout at any time once their balance reaches ₦10,000. Payment is made directly to their registered Nigerian bank account within 3–5 business days. A full payout history and earnings dashboard is available in the tutor portal.
What happens if a session is cancelled?
No commission is charged on cancelled sessions. If a session is cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice by the tutor, the platform may apply a cancellation policy. See our full refund policy for details.
Will the commission rate ever change?
The current rate is 10% and we commit to providing tutors with at least 60 days' notice before any change to the commission structure. Our goal is to remain the most tutor-friendly platform in Nigeria.
