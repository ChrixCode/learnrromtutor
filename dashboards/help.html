Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tutoring session?

Browse our tutors, select one that matches your needs, choose an available time slot from their calendar, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation email with session details.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings from your profile dropdown, select "Change Password," enter your current and new password, and save the changes.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes, you can reschedule sessions up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. Go to My Sessions, find the session, and click "Reschedule."

How do I contact my tutor?

You can send messages to your tutor through the Messages section in your dashboard. Tutors typically respond within 24 hours.
