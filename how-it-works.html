How 2tors.ng Works

Everything you need to find, book and track a verified private tutor in Nigeria — in four simple steps

  1. Home
  2. How It Works

Four Steps to Your First Session

Find a Tutor

Search verified tutors by subject, education level, delivery mode (home visit or online) and location across Nigeria.

Request a Booking

Select your child, choose the subject, your preferred date and time slot, then send a booking request to the tutor.

Attend the Session

The tutor arrives at your home or sends you a video link. Your child attends the confirmed session.

Track Progress

After every session, the tutor submits a structured progress report. You view it instantly in your parent dashboard.

For Parents & Guardians

Everything you need to manage your children's tutoring in one place

Create a Family Account

  • Register in under 2 minutes — no payment required at signup
  • Add all your children as separate profiles, each with their own level, subjects and goals
  • Invite additional guardians (father, aunt, grandparent) with their own permission settings
  • Each guardian can be allowed or restricted from booking, viewing reports or managing camera settings
  • You stay in control at all times — revoke any guardian's access instantly

Find & Book a Verified Tutor

  • Search by subject, education level, state/city, delivery mode and availability
  • Read detailed profiles, qualifications, reviews and ratings from other parents
  • Request a booking for a specific child with your preferred date and time
  • The tutor confirms, declines or proposes an alternative time
  • Once confirmed, the session appears in your calendar automatically

Monitor Progress & Reports

  • After every session, the tutor submits a structured progress report
  • Reports include: session attendance, strengths, areas to improve, homework and next steps
  • You receive a notification as soon as a report is submitted
  • View full history of all reports, assignments and sessions per child
  • Compare progress across subjects and over time

For Tutors & Educators

Get verified, build your profile and start earning from bookings

Register & Get Verified

  • Create your tutor account with your personal and professional details
  • Add your subjects, education levels served, delivery modes and service areas
  • Upload your government-issued ID and qualification certificates
  • Our team manually reviews your documents — usually within 48 hours
  • Once approved, your profile goes live and families can start requesting bookings

Accept Bookings & Teach

  • Receive booking requests in your dashboard — accept, decline or propose an alternative time
  • Your weekly availability calendar keeps everything organised
  • For online sessions, a video link is provided through the platform
  • Record attendance at the start of each session
  • Submit structured progress reports immediately after teaching

For Schools & Institutions

Register your institution and manage tutoring across your whole school in a secure, isolated workspace

Register & Verify

Submit your institution's registration documents and await verification by the 2tors.ng team.

Manage Classes

Create classes, enrol learners, link parent guardians and assign class teachers — all in your school workspace.

Track Progress

View attendance, assignments and progress reports across your entire school. Schools cannot access each other's data.
